# Sections: "## icebreaker", "## deep", "## deeper" and "## wildcard".
# One card per line. A card that starts with "[ambos]" is answered by both
# players; any other question is answered by the player whose turn it is.
# Wildcards are small actions instead of questions. Cards are shown to players
# as they are (in Spanish). Add or change whatever you like.
